# Self-evaluation - CURA

*Liv Sanchez, SDS 0902. Also embedded in the presentation (page 15) and the web exhibit.*

## 1. What was successful about your design?
The concept of CURA was the strongest part of my design. The idea of care for materials, craft, and the environment gave every decision a clear direction. The organic curved floor plan worked well in creating a natural journey through the space, drawing visitors from the retail zone inward to the personalisation studio. The material palette felt cohesive and grounded with limewash plaster, rammed earth, spotted gum, and dried botanicals all reinforcing the concept. The sustainability approach was also a highlight with every material having a documented post-event pathway and a zero landfill target.

## 2. What changes would better communicate your concept and final design?
If I were to make changes I would spend more time rendering the perspectives to better communicate the atmosphere of the space, particularly the warmth of the materials and lighting. A more developed sample board with physical swatches would also have strengthened the presentation of the material palette.

## 3. What were the challenges in translating your concept into a space?
The main challenge was translating an abstract concept into a functional commercial space. Balancing the earthy organic aesthetic with practical requirements like fitting the cashier counter, personalisation bar, sewing station and storage into the inner zone required a lot of plan refinement. Getting the curved boundary between the two zones to feel intentional rather than arbitrary was also difficult to communicate in 2D drawings.

## 4. How would you improve your concept development process next time?
Next time I would start my material research earlier in the process so that material decisions could inform the concept rather than follow it. I would also give myself more time between the initial concept development and the spatial planning stage to ensure the two were more deeply connected from the beginning.
